# 🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

> An end-to-end Business Intelligence project built using Power BI to analyse customer churn, identify high-risk customer segments, and provide strategic business recommendations.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the biggest challenges in the banking industry. Acquiring a new customer is significantly more expensive than retaining an existing one.

This project analyses customer churn using Power BI to answer critical business questions such as:

- Who are our customers?
- Why are customers leaving?
- Which customers generate the highest value?
- How can the bank improve customer retention?

The report transforms raw customer data into interactive dashboards and actionable business insights.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The objective of this project was to identify customer churn patterns and recommend strategies to improve customer retention using Business Intelligence techniques.

# 💡 Key Insights

- Overall customer churn rate is approximately **20.37%**
- Germany exhibits comparatively higher customer churn.
- Customer activity significantly impacts retention.
- Customers with multiple products generate higher financial value.
- Credit score is an important predictor of customer behaviour.

---

# 📈 Strategic Recommendations

- Improve engagement with inactive customers.
- Focus customer retention initiatives in Germany.
- Cross-sell additional banking products.
- Prioritise high-value customers.
- Use predictive analytics to identify customers at risk of churn.
